1 Out-of-Sample Empirical Backtesting Framework

To verify model fidelity and avoid synthetic overconfidence, Climate Lens subjects its projection engine to strict temporal out-of-sample backtesting across all 64 districts using authentic daily NASA POWER satellite assimilations (2001–2025):

Training Window (2001–2015 · 15 Years)

Used exclusively to train robust non-parametric Theil–Sen linear regressions and compute residual dispersion ($\pm 1.96\sigma$). Models were blinded to post-2015 reality.

Held-Out Test Window (2016–2025 · 10 Years)

Unseen ground truth used to evaluate predictions across 64 districts $\times$ 10 test years = 640 out-of-sample evaluations for all 4 primary climate indicators.

3 Interval Calibration Disclosure (Scientific Honesty Protocol)
⚖️ Scientific Integrity Statement: Why Empirical Coverage is 20.2% – 55.0% vs Nominal 95%

A standard Gaussian model assumes a stationary $\pm 1.96\sigma$ envelope covers 95% of future observations. In Bangladesh's real climate, our held-out test achieves only 20.2% to 55.0% coverage. We deliberately report this limitation: the South Asian summer monsoon is non-stationary and prone to chaotic extremes (e.g. 2022 Sylhet deluge, where coverage plunged to 5.0%). To mitigate this, Climate Lens couples Theil–Sen statistics with NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 downscaled physical GCM ensembles to provide dynamic 10th-to-90th percentile uncertainty ribbons.

4 Key Scientific Limitations & Boundaries
1. Spatial Grid Resolution (~50 km)

NASA POWER delivers 0.5° (~55 km) grid points. Small neighboring districts (e.g. Dhaka and Narayanganj) share identical readings; micro-scale urban heat islands cannot be resolved.

2. Exploratory Projections vs Forecasts

Trajectories for 2040/2050 are exploratory scenario projections under IPCC SSP2-4.5/SSP5-8.5. They are NOT deterministic local weather forecasts and do not replace BMD advisories.

6 Additional Scientific Limitations & Confounding Nuance
3. Sensor Homogeneity (2001 Start Baseline)

Dataset initiates in 2001 to coincide with TRMM and MODIS satellite assimilations in NASA POWER, preventing artificial pre-2001 sensor transition jumps.

4. Confounding Drivers in Agriculture & Fish

Rice yields and fish stocks are confounded by HYV seed adoption, deep tube well irrigation, and Farakka water diversion. Correlation does not equal isolated climate causality.
